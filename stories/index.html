Data stories

Interactive stories about well-known datasets, each built with Claude to show how quickly an idea can become a finished, sourced site. Every chart's takeaway is computed from the data, not written by hand.

  • Spelled to Victory

    Vite · Observable Plot · Nov 2026

    Every Scripps National Spelling Bee champion since 1925: winning words that have left everyday English, a field that grew from 9 spellers to 567, a prize that lost most of its value for 60 years, and the former champions who now judge and pronounce. Then hear a winning word, ask for a definition or a sentence, and try to spell it.
